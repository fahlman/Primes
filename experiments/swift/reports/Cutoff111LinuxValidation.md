## Completed checks

Both architectures have `status=passed`, `check_scope=full_suite`, all six requested checks completed, no unrequested checks, and empty stderr for each verifier execution:

- Verify under `-O -sanitize=address` and `-O -whole-module-optimization`: complete prime-array comparisons for every limit −2…2048, larger square boundaries, one million and ten million.
- ExtraVerify under both modes: every limit 2049…30000, 11,382 deduplicated 128-bit boundaries, 500 random limits, 1,561 prime-square limits and 3,654 sparse-group/cleanup boundaries.
- PhaseVerify under both modes: 53,015 partial/full flag checks over 2,305 limits, allocation zeroing and full raw-buffer equality, including padding.
- The normal unchanged runtime image: 78,498 validated primes, positive pass count, at least five seconds, one thread and exact `algorithm=base,faithful=yes,bits=1` tags.

The runtime smoke is compatibility evidence only. It does not establish a Linux/Threadripper throughput gain. Native amd64 names the x86-64 architecture; this run used the Intel CPU recorded below, not Threadripper.
